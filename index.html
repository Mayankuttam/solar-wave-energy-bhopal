Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about rooftop solar feasibility, subsidies, net metering, and warranties in Bhopal.

Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers in Bhopal receive ₹30,000 subsidy for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and a maximum of ₹78,000 for systems 3 kW and above. Solar Wave Energy handles all documentation and portal uploads to ensure the subsidy is deposited straight into your bank account.
Once MPMKVVCL installs the bidirectional net-meter, we upload the commissioning certificate to the National Portal. The central government typically processes and transfers the DBT subsidy directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account within 20 to 30 business days.
A bidirectional meter records both electricity imported from the MPMKVVCL grid and surplus solar power exported to the grid. In your monthly electricity bill, exported units are subtracted from imported units. You only pay for the net difference (or receive credit units carry-forward for the next billing cycle).
A 3 kW solar system requires approximately 240 to 270 sq.ft. of shadow-free rooftop area. If you wish to retain usable walking or recreational space on your terrace, we can build an elevated super-structure (approx. 7–9 feet tall) so your terrace remains fully usable underneath.
Modern Monocrystalline and TopCon Bifacial panels continue to generate electricity even under diffused sunlight and cloudy conditions (approx. 25% to 40% of peak capacity). During monsoon months, you seamlessly draw power from the MPMKVVCL grid, offset by banked solar units accumulated during sunny months.
All solar modules come with a 25-year linear power performance warranty and a 10 to 12-year product manufacturing warranty. On-grid string inverters come with a 5 to 10-year manufacturer warranty. Furthermore, Solar Wave Energy provides 5 years of comprehensive workmanship support.
